## SAY · 1 minute

I am probe 3. The product name is Mejor express. It helps you look at university majors.

It is for high school students aged 14–16. They know what they like. They do not know what to study in university.

How to use: answer a few questions, or click things you like. Click 4 things at most. Green = a good fit. Gold = a small fit. Open a major. First see what you can try this year. If a school name looks light, that school does not have this major.

How we count: look at the things you clicked. How many of them this major also has. This is not a test. This is not about what kind of person you are.

This is only a first look. Not a final choice.

---

**Say this:**

我是探头三。产品名字叫 Mejor express。它帮你看大学专业。

给 14–16 岁高中生用。他们知道自己喜欢什么，但不知道大学要学什么。

怎么用：先回答几个问题，或者自己点喜欢的东西。最多点四个。绿色是比较合适。金色是有一点合适。点开一个专业，先看今年可以试什么。有的学校名字是淡的，因为这个学校没有这个专业。

怎么算：看你点的喜欢里面，这个专业对上了几条。不是考试。也不是说你是哪种人。

这只是先看一看，不是最后决定。

## SAY · 3 minutes

Say 1, then click. Say 2, then click. Slow is OK.

### 1. Who is it for

I am probe 3. The name is Mejor express. For students aged 14–16. At night someone asks: what will you study later? They have likes, but they cannot see the major name. After this, they take 2 or 3 names home.

**Say this:**

我是探头三。名字叫 Mejor express。给 14–16 岁的学生用。晚上有人问：你以后学什么？他们有喜欢的东西，但说不出专业的名字。用完可以带走两三个名字。

### 2. How to use 〔clicks 1–4〕

The first page is a ticket. Click “Start with Nova”. Or skip and see the map. Nova is not a personality test. Click 4 likes at most. Four lines: Logic, Care, World, Make. Green = 70% or more. Gold = 40% to 69%.

**Say this:**

第一页是一张车票。点「从 Nova 开始」。或者跳过，去看地图。Nova 不是性格测试。最多点四个喜欢的。有四条线：Logic、Care、World、Make。绿色是百分之七十以上。金色是百分之四十到六十九。

### 3. How it counts 〔click 5, point at 100%〕

The numbers are simple. Percent = how many likes this major also has, divided by how many likes you clicked.

Example: you click “logic and numbers” and “data and patterns”. Computer Science has both. So it is 100%, green.

**Say this:**

数字很好算。百分比 = 这个专业对上了几条，除以你点了几个喜欢。

举例：你点「逻辑与数字」和「数据与规律」。计算机科学两条都有，所以是百分之百，绿色。

### 4. Schools 〔clicks 6–7〕

These are universities in other countries. Many Chinese students go there. The faculty names come from the school website. If a school has no medical school, the name looks light. We do not make up a faculty. Nova is only a helper. Turn it off, the map still works.

**Say this:**

这些是国外的大学。很多中国学生会去。学院名字来自学校网页。如果这个学校没有这个专业，名字会变淡。我们不乱写。Nova 只是帮手。关掉它，地图还能用。

### 5. A bug I fixed

The home page tried to fit one screen, so the ticket became too small. I put the ticket back to normal size, and put the words on the side.

**Say this:**

首页想放进一个屏幕，车票变得太小。后来我让车票变回原来的大小，说明放在旁边。

### 6. End

This is not a best-university list. These are not universities in mainland China. Take the names. Ask your family or teacher. Try one small thing this year.

**Say this:**

这不是最好大学的名单。也不是中国内地的大学。带走名字，去问家人或老师。今年先试一件小事。

## If the teacher asks

**怎么算的？**  
你点了几个喜欢。这个专业对上几个。对上越多，数字越大。七十以上是绿色。

**为什么有的学校是淡的？**  
这个学校没有这个专业。不是网页坏了。

**这是性格测试吗？**  
不是。Nova 只是帮手。关掉也能用。

**这是最好的大学吗？有中国的大学吗？**  
不是排名。没有中国内地的大学。是国外的大学。

**数据放在哪里？**  
只放在这台电脑里。不用登录。

**GitHub / 网上的链接呢？**  
If you have a link, read it.  
If not, say: **今天先在电脑上给大家看。**
